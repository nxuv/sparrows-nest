### *THE YEAR IS 2392*

In the southern hemisphere of Alpha Proxima IV, a fledgling polity known as the Republic of Maiombe is on its way to celebrate its first decade as an independent country free from major conflicts - whether against overreaching neighbors with colonial ambitions or ideologues-turned-warlords at the behest of foreign capital.

### *FOR ONCE, THINGS ARE LOOKING UP.*

Since multiple decades at war have left its coffers nearly empty, there past nine years have coincided with a gradual loosening of economic policy, alongside a tentative opening to parliamentary politics - which has allowed the previous ruling uniparty to once again keep a hand on the levers of power, now as leaders of a ruling coalition. While many grumbled at the news, this arrangement has mostly worked out, setting the conditions for an ongoing period of national renewal best exemplified in the glowup received by the country's capital city, the seaside oasis of Nova Luanda.

### *BUT THAT'S NOT WHERE YOU'RE GOING.*

As an operative for Clear Paths, a paramilitary NGO specialized in peacetime drone-hunting, you'll be doing south: knee-deep into former rebel territories. There, you'll be assisting the Maiombese army in the culling of bipedal biomechs that were gifted to the rebels by their favored foreign partner: the United Systems Alliance. Biomechs which may be developing a form of "machine rampancy", something a weapons platform shouldn't even be able to develop due to stringent Virtual Intelligence laws.

### *SO KEEP YOUR EYES PEELED, AND MAKE NOTE OF ALL YOU SEE.*

Inspired by [CHVLR](https://susanahgrace.itch.io/chvlr), [Ballistic.exe](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3432980/Ballisticexe/) and Metal Gear Solid V, Maiombe Tank Safari is a solo journaling game in which you take the role of a drone hunter on a mostly-solitary mission to observe, make note and ultimately cull individual models of biomechanical combat drones left running on autopilot years after the end of the war they were fighting ended.

Your playthrough (or "run") will be split into days: ever day you'll be rolling how many events happen to you on a given day and drawing cards from a playing cards deck to determine the nature of each one- ranging from untapped streams and air support runs, to rival mercenaries, corporate killers and unrelenting clouds of alien mosquitos.

Once you draw an ace, you'll have to choose between taking the shot or tailing your quarry for an easier shot next time you get the chance. Whenever you decide to shoot, you may resolve the action by simply rolling a D6 and checking your score against the target board - or by pinning it up to a real metal target and shooting it with a real shooting instrument of your choice, whether it's a BB gun, a foam dart or an actual bullet.
