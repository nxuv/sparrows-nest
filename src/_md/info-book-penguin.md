### *The year is 1939*

Nine years after after the failure of a drilling expedition from the university of Boston-Miskatonic, the freezing wastes of Antarctica are once again intruded upon by men of the north, seeking to uncover what lies underneath mankind's last land to conquer. It is the Deutsche Antarktische Expedition, helmed by Dr. [REDACTED] Weltenfrei and Brigadier General [REDACTED].

### *Lured by the call of a glorious past*

Plodding across the snow, the young troopers making up the expedition's muscle came across the remains of a diary. An old thing, seemingly belonging to their predecessors from Boston-Miskatonic. Once a basecamp had been set up, this diary started changing hands, puzzling the soldiers with its strange photographs and puzzling scribbles, written down by shaking hands in a language few but Dr Weltenfrei even understood: American English.

### *Foreknowledge of an impending doom, daring the next bunch of fools to step to it*

The contents of this diary matched with what nuggets of forbidden knowledge Dr. Weltenfrei had accumulated on previous expeditions. And while fully deciphering its contents may have had adverse effects on troop morale, the document itself would have been a valuable addition to the Reich's archives, or even the Deutsches Museum back in Berlin - so it was stamped for express delivery and stashed away.

### *It never made it to Berlin, but someone else did find it - and now, it's in your hands*

**Penguin Colony: The Field Notes** is a 40-page zine-journal detailing the last days of a doomed antarctic expedition predating the events of **Penguin Colony**, the latest in avian-based, weird fiction platforming by **Origame Digital**.

All pictures contained therein were taken using Penguin Colony's **in-game camera**, while the diary entries captioning them were actually **hand-written**, in order to achieve the most accurate possible depiction of a frost-bitten scientist hurriedly scribbling on his notebook as he marches to his doom.

This zine comes in two versions: a regular version entirely produced Data Sparrow, and a **"Director's Edit"** version by **Nephtali Faulkner**, the director of Penguin Colony. The regular version is in full color and contains **6 extra pages of pdf-fillable notes**, while the "Director's Edit" version is in black and white, contains additional pages of **map data** as well as notes, and is already laid out for staple-bound, DIY printing.
