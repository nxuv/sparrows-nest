Somewhere in the eastern kingdoms, tucked away in the evergreen forests, rumors spoke of an accursed fortress hiding riches beyond this world. Nobody's ever seen it of course, but adventurers from far and wide came to look for it - only to never turn up again. And while most believed them to be lost, whether kidnapped by bandits or consumed by the local fauna, the rumors kept spreading for decades on end.

As you push past the rusted gate, you find yourself ruing the day you gave credence to such rumors. The months and years they spent burrowing into your mind, until something stirring inside you pushed you to set out and seek the fortress yourself. You unexpectedly succeeded, enduring many perils along the way.

Unfortunately, as you peer into the halls of this great ruin, a disconcerting thought grows into your chest. You feel it tighten under your breastplate, as you realize that...

**Solar Curse** is a very short dungeon crawler inspired by King's Field, powered by a **homegrown software renderer** for the **1.44MB GAMEDEV CONTEST**: a game jam challenging game developers across the world to make any kind of videogame-shaped software that could fit in a single floppy disk.

It may be developed into a full game at some point in the future, in which case this page will get a revamp too.
